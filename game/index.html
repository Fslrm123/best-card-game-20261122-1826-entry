AETHERFALL
DUELS

Three lanes. Twenty life. One Voidwarden between you and the dawn.

How to Play

  • Goal: reduce the Voidwarden's Life from 20 to 0 before he does the same to you.
  • Aether: you gain +1 max Aether each turn (up to 10) and it refills every turn. Cards cost Aether, shown in the blue gem.
  • Play: click a card in your hand, then click an empty lane on your side to summon it. Spells: click the card, then click a target.
  • Attack: creatures wake up the turn after they are summoned. Click a ready creature — it strikes the creature opposite it, or the enemy hero if that lane is empty.
  • Keys: 1–5 select a card in hand, Enter ends your turn.

An original card duel · no downloads, no accounts — just you, your deck, and the dark.
